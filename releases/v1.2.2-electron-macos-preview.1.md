# SoundShredder 1.2.2 — signed macOS Electron preview

**Candidate for the final Finder installation test.** Both Apple Silicon and Intel builds passed Developer ID signing, Apple notarization, stapled-ticket validation and Gatekeeper assessment. The automated tests also passed on the app copied from the DMG. A normal browser download, drag to Applications and Finder launch on a personal Mac still need verification before this draft is published.

SoundShredder keeps the dark/mint workspace in its own Mac window, with video preview, dialogue/music/effects tracks, bubble cleanup and audio exports. This update adds a storage folder picker, clearer setup progress, cancellation and recovery, and reliable close/reopen behavior.

## Install, then reopen whenever you need it

1. Quit the older app, keeping its engine and session folders.
2. Open the matching DMG, drag SoundShredder into **Applications**, and eject the disk image. For a ZIP, extract the app and move it into Applications.
3. Open SoundShredder from Applications. Python is bundled; no Homebrew or separate Python installation is needed.
4. Optionally select **Choose folder…** for engines, models and sessions, then select **Set up SoundShredder**. Compatible completed engines are reused.
5. Drop in audio or video, choose a cleanup preset, compare the cleaned and removed audio, and export the mix or isolated tracks.
6. Quit with **Cmd+Q**, then reopen from Applications. Saved sessions and the selected storage location should remain available.

Initial setup needs internet and at least **3 GiB free for the engine**, plus room for the app, models and media. Layer separation downloads approximately **426 MB** of models; Bubble FX downloads approximately **1.2 GB**. Cached engines and models work offline, and audio processing stays on your computer.

Changing storage locations leaves existing files where they are. An empty location starts a separate setup; selecting the original SoundShredder folder returns to its sessions. A disconnected drive opens a recovery screen with retry and folder-selection options. Setup includes progress details, cancellation and repair after interruption.

- Developer ID signatures, native-code integrity, Apple notarization, stapled tickets and Gatekeeper acceptance for the actual DMG and ZIP contents.
- CPU engine setup, model download, real audio separation and WAV export from the DMG-installed app.
- Video playback and its toggle, duplicate launches, saved sessions, and four close/reopen cycles including a simulated app crash.
- Setup cancellation/retry, storage folder selection and disconnected-drive recovery.
- Signature and notarization checks again after processing and relaunch, confirming the installed app remained intact.

**Remaining check:** download through a normal browser on a personal Mac, install into Applications, launch from Finder, process a short clip, quit and reopen. Keep standard macOS security settings. The usual confirmation for an app downloaded from the internet is expected; removing quarantine or disabling Gatekeeper is not an installation step. Broader hardware performance and subjective cleanup quality remain outside these automated checks.

The older `v1.2.0-electron-macos-preview.1` files remain unsigned and unnotarized and retain their known Finder launch issue. This candidate does not replace those files in place.

For updates, storage details, SSL troubleshooting and uninstall instructions, see the attached **MACOS-INSTALL.md**. To uninstall the app, quit and move SoundShredder.app to Trash; engines, models, sessions and exported audio remain until you remove them separately.
